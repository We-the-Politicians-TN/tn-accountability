What this is, and what it is not

This site does not accuse anyone of anything. It shows patterns in public records and gives you the records. Deciding whether any pattern matters is your job, and determining whether any rule was broken belongs to the Registry of Election Finance, the Ethics Commission, and the courts.

Timing alone tells you nothing — and we can show you why

Tennessee bars contributions while the General Assembly is in session. In 2025 legislators received {{ '$1,385,084' }} in January and {{ '$1,700' }} in February. Almost every member therefore raises money in the same few months, and almost every bill has money behind it in the preceding weeks.

So "this legislator received money shortly before introducing a bill" describes nearly the entire General Assembly. We do not treat it as meaningful on its own. What we look at instead is whether the money came from the same industry the bill concerns. Read the method.
