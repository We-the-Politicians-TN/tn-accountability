Travel and expenses paid by others

Every year each member files a Statement of Disclosure of Interests under penalty of perjury. Question 8 asks for two things: Part A, any private contribution used to defray the expenses of their legislative duties; Part B, any travel paid for by "a person with an interest in Tennessee state public policy" to inform or advise them. This is the one benefit channel Tennessee discloses by member, so it can be shown against a name. Here is every entry, as filed.

How to read this

"None reported" is the norm: most members report nothing here in most years. An entry is a member stating, on the record, who paid for what. It is not an allegation, and there are ordinary reasons for each — conferences, study trips, legislative organisations. Travel paid by a government body or a recognised organisation of public officials need not be disclosed and so does not appear.
