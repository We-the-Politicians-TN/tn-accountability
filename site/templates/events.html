Who hosts the legislature

Tennessee bars lobbyists and their employers from giving legislators gifts. What the law allows is narrow: an event to which the entire General Assembly is invited, capped at a set cost per person per day, reported to the Ethics Commission within thirty days. Every such event since 2006 is listed here.

What this can and cannot tell you

These records name the host and the cost. They never record who attended — the legal condition is that everyone was invited, so no attendance exists. An event here says an organisation spent money hosting the legislature. It says nothing about any individual member, and we do not link it to one.
